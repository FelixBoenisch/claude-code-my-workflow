## Coverage limits

Author order, titles, bibliographic years, publication venues, volumes/issues, pagination or article identifiers, DOI and entry type/status were compared where applicable. A source's abbreviated byline is not treated as proof that a full given name or middle initial is wrong. Article identifiers remain compatible with the bibliography's existing pages-field convention. Citation keys can remain unchanged even when a year is harmonized. Published journal versions already present in these 30 entries were retained; there are no remaining working-paper-type entries in this assignment to convert.
