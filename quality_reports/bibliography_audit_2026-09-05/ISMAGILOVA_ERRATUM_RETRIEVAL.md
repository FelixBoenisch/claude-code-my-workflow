## What it changes for Ismagilova and Ploner

The notice says that ethics approval and/or informed-consent statements were omitted from the published versions of the listed volume 4 articles and that statements supplied by their authors have now been added. Ismagilova and Ploner's article is item **7**, beginning at the bottom right of erratum page 1; its statement is at the top left of page 2.

For this paper, the correction adds an **Ethical Statement**. It explains that formal ethics approval was unnecessary under the University of Trento committee's guidelines because participants were anonymous adults facing no identifiable risk. Prolific participants were informed about the study, its duration, and compensation; participation was voluntary, starting after reviewing the information constituted consent, and withdrawal remained possible.

The full article-specific statement, transcribed from the locally supplied PDF, is:

> As per the guidelines of the Ethical Committee of the University of Trento, formal ethics approval was not required as the study involved anonymous adult participants who faced no identifiable risk. The experiment was hosted on Prolific, where participants were informed of the study's nature, duration, and compensation. Participation was voluntary, and consent was obtained by virtue of participants opting to begin the study after reviewing the study specific information that was provided. Participants are free to withdraw at any time.

The same statement is already present on printed page 9 of the locally supplied original article. The correction to this paper does not revise its findings, data, analyses, or bibliographic details. The bibliography entry can remain **Ismagilova and Ploner (2025), volume 4, article 100147, DOI 10.1016/j.chbah.2025.100147**. No additional bibliography entry is needed unless the manuscript discusses the erratum itself.
