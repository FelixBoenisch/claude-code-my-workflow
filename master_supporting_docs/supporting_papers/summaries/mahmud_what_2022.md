# Mahmud, Islam, Ahmed & Smolander (2022) — *What influences algorithmic decision-making?*

## Type

**Systematic literature review** of 80 empirical studies on algorithm aversion, identified through database search and snowballing.

## Research question

What factors influence algorithm aversion? The paper synthesises empirical findings into a comprehensive framework organising the moderators of algorithm acceptance/rejection.

## Methods

- Systematic literature review across 7 academic databases.
- 80 empirical studies on algorithm aversion.
- Inductive thematic categorisation.

## Four-theme framework for influencing factors

1. **Algorithm factors**: characteristics of the algorithm itself (accuracy, transparency, anthropomorphism, modifiability).
2. **Individual factors**: user characteristics (expertise, personality, trust propensity, demographic).
3. **Task factors**: characteristics of the decision domain (objectivity, complexity, moral content, uncertainty).
4. **High-level factors**: contextual / organisational / societal factors (incentives, regulations, social norms).

## Main findings

1. **Algorithm aversion is heterogeneous** — factors at four different levels jointly determine acceptance.
2. **Algorithm and individual factors are well-studied**.
3. **Task and high-level factors are under-studied** — gap in the literature.
4. **Recommendations for future research** on task and high-level moderators.
