# European Union (2024) — *Artificial Intelligence Act*

## Type

**Legal regulation** — the EU's comprehensive AI regulatory framework. Not academic literature; a binding legal instrument.

## Purpose

To establish a **uniform legal framework** for the development, placement on the market, putting into service, and use of AI systems in the EU. The Act aims to:

- Promote **human-centric and trustworthy AI**.
- Ensure a high level of protection of health, safety, fundamental rights, democracy, rule of law, environmental protection.
- Protect against harmful effects of AI systems.
- Support innovation.
- Ensure free movement of AI-based goods and services within the EU.

## Key features (high-level)

- **Risk-based approach**: AI systems classified by risk level (unacceptable risk, high risk, limited risk, minimal risk).
- **Prohibited practices**: certain AI use cases banned outright (e.g., social scoring by public authorities, real-time biometric identification in public spaces with narrow exceptions).
- **High-risk systems**: subject to obligations including risk-management, data governance, transparency, human oversight, accuracy, robustness, cybersecurity, conformity assessment.
- **Transparency obligations** for certain AI systems (chatbots, deepfakes, emotion recognition).
- **Penalties** for non-compliance: fines up to €35 million or 7% of global turnover.
