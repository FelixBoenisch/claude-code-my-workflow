# Trunk, Birkel & Hartmann (2020) — *On the current state of combining human and artificial intelligence for strategic organizational decision making*

## Type

**Systematic literature review** combined with content analysis. Synthesis paper, not empirical.

## Research question

How can AI be integrated into **strategic organizational decision-making under uncertainty**? What are the challenges, preconditions, and consequences that need to be considered?

## Methods

- Systematic literature review of research on human-AI decision making in organisational/strategic contexts.
- Content analysis of relevant papers.
- Synthesises findings into a conceptual model.

## Main findings

1. **AI offers support for decision-making under uncertainty** in organisations, but research is still developing.
2. **AI capabilities differ from traditional machines** — the technology has implications for division of tasks and role definitions.
3. **Three areas of relatively-developed research**:
   - Organisational structures for human-AI collaboration.
   - Choice of AI applications.
   - Knowledge management.
4. **Underdeveloped area: ethical frameworks** — flagged as crucial foundation but missing.
5. **Counterintuitive insight**: AI can *amplify* problems in decision-making (e.g., bias propagation) rather than reduce them.
6. **Human responsibility increases** with AI use, requiring new education frameworks.

**(2) The "human responsibility increases" finding aligns with the manuscript's H1 reversal.** Trunk et al. argue that AI use *increases* rather than reduces human responsibility.
