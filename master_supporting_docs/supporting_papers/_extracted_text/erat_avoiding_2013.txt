31  People do not always lie, even when lying increases their monetary payos. Still, even when
32

33  lying is aversive, can hiring someone to lie for you allow a person to avoid the disutility from

34

35  lying, while at the same time ensuring higher payos? The current article investigates this

36  empirical question - the possibility of delegated deception - through a laboratory experiment.

37

38  The results indicate that a signicant fraction of people employ an agent (to lie) even when

39

40  they could lie themselves. Moreover, the likelihood of delegating to an agent depends on the

41  incentives, with more people choosing to delegate when the lie hurts to a greater extent the

42

43  person being lied to. Finally, analysis of gender dierences in the tendency to use an agent
44

45  revealed that that women are more likely to delegate to an agent compared to men, especially

46

47  so when the harm inicted by the lie is larger.

15  In many economic interactions, such as selling a used car, honesty might not necessarily be the best

16

17  policy, at least if the goal is to maximize monetary payos. In situations such as these, one is faced

18

19  with the following choice: engage in deception and improve one's pecuniary benets while reducing

20

21  the other's payos, or tell the truth and forgo one's own benets. In an empirical study of how

22

23  people make this choice, Gneezy (2005) nds that people do not always lie even when lying increases

24

25  one's own payos. And more interestingly, the choice of whether or not to engage in deception is

26

27  sensitive to incentives, with people being more likely to lie if their own benet is higher and/or

28  other's loss is lower.
29

31  The vast majority of extant economics literature focuses on settings where a person might

32  undertake one of only two actions (for an interesting article that expands the action space, see

33

34  Sanchez-Pages and Vorsatz, 2009). The moral one, where the person tells the truth; Or the immoral

35

36  one, where the person tells a lie. But, at least in some situations, these two actions are not the only

37

38  two available ones. For instance, while (most?) people may be averse to hiring an illegal immigrant

39

41

42  choose to hire only legal workers. An alternative course of action might be to hire an agent to

43

44  act on your behalf, with the possibility that this person might engage in actions that you yourself

45

46  would not do.
